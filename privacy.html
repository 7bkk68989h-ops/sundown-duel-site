Privacy Policy — Sundown Duel

Last updated: 18 September 2026

Sundown Duel is a game made by Pedro Pinto ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game stores, what other players can see, and what we never collect. It applies to Sundown Duel on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV.

The short version: the game has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It stores nothing about you unless you play online.

What stays on your device

These settings are saved on the device and never leave it:

  • the cowboy outfit you have chosen;
  • whether the music is on or off;
  • the language you picked;
  • your progress in the single-player career.

Deleting the app deletes them.

What is stored when you play online

Online play is built on Apple's Game Center and on a public iCloud (CloudKit) database that belongs to this game. When you open the online part of the game, three things are written there so that other players can find you and challenge you:

  • A player key. This is not your Game Center identifier: it is an irreversible hash of it, made so that the same person is recognised across sessions without the identifier itself being stored.
  • Your Game Center nickname, as Game Center reports it.
  • The country of your device region, shown as a flag. This comes from the device's region setting, not from GPS or any location service. The game never asks for, receives or stores your location.

The same three pieces of information are stored when you win an online match, so that the online tables of the Hall of Fame can be shown.

Other players can see this information. The list of players who are online, the challenges and the online Hall of Fame all show the nickname and the flag to everyone playing the game. Nothing else about you is shown, and nothing you write is transmitted: the game has no chat. During a match, players can only send each other a small set of fixed emoji.

Presence records disappear on their own shortly after you stop playing. Records of online wins are kept for as long as the tables exist.

What the game never collects

  • No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind.
  • No analytics, no third-party trackers, no software development kits that collect data.
  • No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data.
  • No payment details (see below).
  • No data is ever shared with other companies, and nothing the game stores is used to track you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Cowboy outfits are in-app purchases handled entirely by the App Store. Payment happens between you and Apple: we never see or receive your payment details. The game asks the App Store which outfits you own, and stores nothing about your purchases on any server of ours. Restoring purchases on another device asks the App Store the same question.

Game Center and iCloud

Signing in to Game Center is optional and only needed for online play. Game Center and iCloud are Apple services, and Apple's handling of your data there is covered by Apple's own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

If you allow it, the game asks Game Center for your friends list, for the single purpose of marking which of the players currently online are friends of yours. The list is not stored and never leaves your device.

Children

The game is not directed at young children, has no chat and no user-written text. We do not knowingly collect information from children beyond the nickname and country described above, which come from the Game Center account signed in on the device.

Your rights

You can stop everything described here by not playing online, or by signing out of Game Center on your device.

If you want the information linked to your player key removed — for example your nickname and country in the online Hall of Fame — write to the address below from the email account of your Apple ID, and it will be deleted. You may also ask what is stored about you. For players in the European Union, these are your rights of access and erasure under the GDPR.
