For help, feedback, purchase issues, or to report abuse, email apps.iloveideas@gmail.com. We reply within two business days.

Tournament chat and reporting abuse

Online tournaments include a chat. Every message passes through a filter that blocks offensive language in all the languages the game supports.

If a message is abusive, tap it in the chat:

  • Mute hides every message from that player on your device.
  • Report mutes the player and sends the message to us for review.

We review reports within 24 hours. Objectionable messages are removed and repeat offenders are blocked. You can also report abuse by email at the address above, with the tournament and the player's name.

In-app purchases

Outfits are bought through the App Store. To restore them on a new device, open Options → Outfits → Restore Purchases. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Privacy

Online play uses Game Center. Tournament data, chat messages and reports are stored in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit). We don't collect any other personal data. See the privacy policy.
