Privacy Policy — Sundown Duel Last updated: 30 September 2026 Sundown Duel is a game made by Pedro Pinto ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game stores, what other players can see, and what we never collect. It applies to Sundown Duel on iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. The short version: the game has no accounts of its own, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It stores nothing about you unless you play online. What stays on your device These settings are saved on the device and never leave it: the cowboy outfit you have chosen; whether the music is on or off; the language you picked; your progress in the single-player career. Deleting the app deletes them. What is stored when you play online Online play is built on Apple's Game Center and on a public iCloud (CloudKit) database that belongs to this game. When you open the online part of the game, three things are written there so that other players can find you and challenge you: A player key. This is not your Game Center identifier: it is an irreversible hash of it, made so that the same person is recognised across sessions without the identifier itself being stored. Your Game Center nickname, as Game Center reports it. The country of your device region, shown as a flag. This comes from the device's region setting, not from GPS or any location service. The game never asks for, receives or stores your location. The same three pieces of information are stored when you win an online match, so that the online tables of the Hall of Fame can be shown. Other players can see this information. The list of players who are online, the challenges and the online Hall of Fame all show the nickname and the flag to everyone playing the game. Nothing else about you is shown. During a match, players can only send each other a small set of fixed emoji. Tournaments, chat and statistics If you join an online tournament, your player key, nickname and flag are stored with the tournament bracket, its results and any medals you win, and are shown to everyone who follows the tournament. Tournaments have a chat. Messages you write are stored in the game's iCloud database with your nickname and are visible to the other players in that tournament. Every message passes through an offensive-language filter, and messages that fail it are never sent or stored. Chat messages are kept only while the tournament exists. Tapping another player's message lets you mute that player (on your device only) or report the message. A report stores the message, its author's player key and nickname, and your player key, so that we can review it. Reports are reviewed within 24 hours, objectionable messages are removed and repeat offenders are blocked. Reports are deleted once handled. When you play alone against the computer or online, the game also keeps your statistics (matches played and won, accuracy, eagles and snake bites) on your device and, if you use Game Center, linked to your player key so that other players can see them. Game Center achievements are handled by Apple. Presence records disappear on their own shortly after you stop playing. Records of online wins are kept for as long as the tables exist. What the game never collects No advertising identifiers, and no advertising of any kind. No analytics, no third-party trackers, no software development kits that collect data. No location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data. No payment details (see below). No data is ever shared with other companies, and nothing the game stores is used to track you across other apps or websites. Purchases Cowboy outfits are in-app purchases handled entirely by the App Store. Payment happens between you and Apple: we never see or receive your payment details. The game asks the App Store which outfits you own, and stores nothing about your purchases on any server of ours. Restoring purchases on another device asks the App Store the same question. Game Center and iCloud Signing in to Game Center is optional and only needed for online play. Game Center and iCloud are Apple services, and Apple's handling of your data there is covered by Apple's own privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ If you allow it, the game asks Game Center for your friends list, for the single purpose of marking which of the players currently online are friends of yours. The list is not stored and never leaves your device. Children The game is not directed at young children. The only text players write is the filtered tournament chat described above. We do not knowingly collect information from children beyond what is described above: the nickname and country come from the Game Center account signed in on the device, and the chat messages are the ones they choose to write. Your rights You can stop everything described here by not playing online, or by signing out of Game Center on your device. If you want the information linked to your player key removed — for example your nickname and country in the online Hall of Fame — write to the address below from the email account of your Apple ID, and it will be deleted. You may also ask what is stored about you. For players in the European Union, these are your rights of access and erasure under the GDPR.
